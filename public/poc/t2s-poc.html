Speech-to-Text POC

Transcribe an audio file or a video's audio track into SRT subtitles. Inference runs in this browser tab; no audio is uploaded to a Dionysus server or sent to a transcription API.

1. Choose media

Audio files are decoded locally. For video, the browser records its audio track during real-time playback before transcription (keep this tab open). Video audio capture depends on browser support; Chrome is recommended. Very long files may require substantial memory.

3. SRT output

1. Load existing subtitles and audio

Uses local Whisper segment timestamps as acoustic anchors and matches them against your SRT. Word/character timing is estimated within each Whisper segment; this is not phoneme-level forced alignment. Results depend on the subtitle text matching the spoken audio.
