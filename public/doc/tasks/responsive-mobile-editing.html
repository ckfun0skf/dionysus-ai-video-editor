DYS-012 · M4 — Preview, compositing, and mobile editing

Responsive mobile editing

Planned · Specification

Goal

Make the editing workspace practical on phone and tablet screens while preserving Storyboard, References, and Assets navigation.

Scope

  • Mobile-first reflow of preview, timeline, and Inspector.
  • Touch-friendly clip selection, playhead scrubbing, and version controls.
  • Keep desktop behavior and keyboard shortcuts intact.
  • Test portrait, landscape, and tablet breakpoints.

Acceptance criteria

  • No horizontal page overflow at supported mobile widths.
  • Preview and timeline are both accessible without losing selection.
  • Controls have practical touch targets and visible focus states.
  • Storyboard, References, and Assets remain available.
  • Responsive checks and manual smoke tests are recorded.

Verification evidence

Record viewport sizes, browser/device, screenshots, overflow checks, and tested editing flows before marking complete.
