DYS-011 · M3 — Timeline model and Version workflow

Multi-track Timeline and linked audio

Planned · Specification

Goal

Support multiple video tracks and audio that follows its source video by default, with the ability to unlink audio and edit it independently.

Scope

  • Explicit video/audio timeline items with track, timing, and source references.
  • Link source audio to video by default when available.
  • Unlink audio and independently move, trim, split, or delete it.
  • Preserve relationships through import/export and undo/redo.

Acceptance criteria

  • Project schema distinguishes video tracks, audio tracks, and clip relationships.
  • Unlinking audio allows independent edits without moving video.
  • Invalid ranges/references are rejected; valid edits survive JSON round-trip.
  • Automated tests cover linked movement, unlinking, independent edits, deletion, and malformed data.

Verification evidence

Before marking complete, attach test names/results, a JSON fixture demonstrating linked and unlinked audio, and a browser walkthrough.
