DYS-011 · M3 — Timeline model and Version workflow

Multi-track Timeline and linked audio

In progress · implementation slice

Goal

Show source audio as an expandable child lane beneath its video layer. Unlink detaches the audio into an independently editable and independently muteable Audio Layer.

Scope

  • Render linked source audio as an indented child lane under its video layer.
  • Unlink without changing the current timing, then move/trim/delete the audio independently.
  • Provide a persistent Mute/Unmute control on every Audio Layer.
  • Never display a generic Media track; preserve link, mute, and timing state through history and JSON.

Acceptance criteria

  • Expanding a video layer reveals its linked audio child lane.
  • Unlink leaves current audio/video timing unchanged and enables independent editing.
  • Muting one audio layer leaves every other audio layer unaffected.
  • Invalid references are rejected; links and mute state survive Undo/Redo and JSON round-trip.
  • Automated model/integration tests cover linked movement, unlink, mute isolation, deletion, and malformed data.

Verification evidence

Before marking complete, attach test names/results, a JSON fixture demonstrating linked and unlinked audio, and deterministic test/build evidence. Automated browser smoke tests are not required unless explicitly requested by the user.
